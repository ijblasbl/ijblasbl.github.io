01The association

Opportunity starts with connection.

Internationaler Jugend- und Bildungsverein Luxembourg a.s.b.l. is a nonprofit association supporting young people, learning and cooperation in Luxembourg and internationally.

Our purpose is to encourage personal, social and professional development, intercultural understanding and active participation in society. We place particular emphasis on young people and people with fewer opportunities to participate.

✳

More opportunities to learn. More ways to take part. More connections across cultures.

02Our focus

Small beginnings. Meaningful possibilities.

These are the areas that guide our work and the initiatives we aim to develop, grounded in our founding purpose.

01 /

Learning & youth development

Workshops, training and educational resources that support the personal, social and professional development of young people and volunteers.

02 /

Culture & exchange

Youth exchanges, cultural activities and community gatherings that encourage dialogue and understanding across backgrounds.

03 /

Inclusion & participation

Social and community initiatives with a particular focus on people who face barriers to taking part.

04 /

Environment & digital projects

Educational, environmental and digital initiatives, alongside studies, publications and shared project outcomes.

↗

We aim to work with schools, universities, public bodies, associations, foundations and other partners in Luxembourg and abroad.
