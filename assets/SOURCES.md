Activity titles and short descriptions are based on the captions of those posts. French and German descriptions are translations. The website does not infer project dates, funding awards, participant counts, venues or partnerships from the photographs. The source links let visitors read the full original posts.

The language café photo is also used as the homepage banner. The official logo is used unchanged in the header and footer; `favicon.png` is a reduced-resolution PNG copy of that logo. The association owner specified `ijblasbl@gmail.com` as the public contact email.
